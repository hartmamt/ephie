Game Setup

How to Play:

  • Objective: Be the first to play all your cards by forming melds!
  • Sets: 3+ cards of the same number, different colors
  • Runs: 3+ consecutive numbers of the same color
  • Turn: Draw a card, optionally play melds, then discard
  • Special Cards: Skip, Reverse, Draw, Wild, and FLIP!
  • FLIP: Changes all cards to their opposite side (Light ↔ Dark)
