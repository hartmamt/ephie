How to Play (Hybrid UNO + Rummy):

  • Objective: First to empty your hand wins!
  • Your Turn - Choose ONE:
    • 📥 Draw a card (if you can't/won't play)
    • 🎯 Play card(s) matching color/number (UNO-style)
    • 🃏 Play a meld (3+ cards: Set or Run)
  • Card Matching: Cards must match discard pile's color OR number
  • Melds (Rummy): Set (same #, diff colors) or Run (consecutive #s, same color)
  • Special Cards: Skip, Reverse, Draw, Wild, FLIP - play like UNO!
  • FLIP Card: Switches deck between Light ↔ Dark side!
